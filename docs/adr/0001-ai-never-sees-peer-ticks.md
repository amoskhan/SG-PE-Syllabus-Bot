# The AI never sees pupils' peer assessment ticks

When the AI analyses a clip, it judges from the video alone and is never given the cues an assessor ticked. Pupils' ticks are often wrong, and an AI that can see a confident "YES" tends to agree with it, so letting it read them would blur the line between what the video shows and what a child believed. The ticks are still kept as evidence for the teacher's own assessment. No model is trained or tuned on them.

## Considered Options

- **Show the ticks so the AI can point out where a pupil and the video disagree.** This was rejected: it's useful for teaching, but it means the AI's judgement can no longer be trusted to come from the video.
