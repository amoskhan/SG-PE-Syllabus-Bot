# Pupil devices show the planned names at check-in

Pupils pick their own names when they check in, instead of the device staying anonymous and the teacher matching names to pairs by hand. Until now a pupil device never saw a name, so nothing confirmed which children were on which iPad, and work landed under the wrong student whenever pupils sat in the wrong pair. We accept that the shared device now shows names, within tight limits: only the students in the lesson's Pair Assignment, by their short names, only through the lesson pass for a lesson dated today, and only at check-in. The device keeps the chosen students for that lesson, not the list of names. Each name can be taken once per lesson; the teacher frees a name taken by mistake, and the teacher's Pair Assignment is still the plan the names are checked against.

## Considered Options

- **Stay anonymous, teacher matches names to pairs** (as before): rejected, it is a job every lesson and nothing checks the children on the device.
- **A PIN or picture password per pupil**: rejected, too hard for P1 pupils; the teacher in the room and names taken once per lesson are the guard.
- **Typed names for pupils not on the list**: rejected, a pupil device must never create a student record; the teacher adds missing children.
