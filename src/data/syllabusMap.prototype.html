Syllabus map prototype

Question: can the app turn the 2024 syllabus into a short map, one line per section, that a teacher finds readable, that fits about 6,000 tokens, and that still shows where things sit (e.g. "kicking starts at P2")? Each line is cut from the real syllabus text with no AI involved. Tweak the settings, search the map, and open any line to compare it with the syllabus wording. Throwaway prototype for issue #130.
